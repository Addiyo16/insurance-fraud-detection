# HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY CONTRACT

## Section 1: Inpatient Hospitalization Coverage
1.1 Room and Board expenses are covered up to $5,000 per day for normal wards.
1.2 Intensive Care Unit (ICU) charges are covered up to a maximum of $15,000 per day, subject to medical necessity.
1.3 All claims are subject to a standard 10% co-pay for non-network hospitals. Network hospitals feature 0% co-pay.

## Section 2: Exclusions & Limitations
2.1 Cosmetic or aesthetic surgeries of any description are strictly excluded from coverage.
2.2 Hospitalization for diagnostic or evaluation purposes only, without active medical treatment, is excluded.
2.3 Any billing for inpatient admission where the hospitalization period is under 24 hours (unless emergency) is not covered.
2.4 Claims submitted from medical providers officially flagged on the insurer's blacklist or suspended registry shall be rejected.

## Section 3: Claim Contestability & Verification
3.1 All medical claims are subject to identity verification. The patient name on all discharge summaries and bills must exactly match the policyholder registry.
3.2 Any discrepancy between billing documents and submitted claims will trigger an automatic fraud audit and potential rejection.
