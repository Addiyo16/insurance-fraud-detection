# LOAN PROTECT INSURANCE POLICY CONTRACT

## Section 1: Coverage Limits
1.1 The policy covers the outstanding principal loan amount in the event of default due to insured contingencies.
1.2 The claim amount is capped at the registered loan contract amount. Over-claiming is strictly prohibited.

## Section 2: Exclusions & Underwriting Limits
2.1 Claims linked to borrowers with credit scores below 500 at underwriting or showing high financial distress (DTI > 60%) are subject to strict fraud scoring.
2.2 Discrepancies between income declarations and credit bureau records represent misrepresentation and are grounds for claim denial.
2.3 The lending contract must be registered under the policy. Unregistered loan IDs are excluded from coverage.

## Section 3: Audits & Verification
3.1 The borrower's monthly income must be verified via bank statements and tax records.
3.2 Claims submitted within 12 months of policy start with claim ratios exceeding 80% are flagged for manual forensic audits.
