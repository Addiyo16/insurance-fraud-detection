# TERM LIFE INSURANCE POLICY CONTRACT

## Section 1: Death Benefit Cover
1.1 The company agrees to pay the Sum Assured to the designated beneficiary upon receipt of proof of death of the Insured, subject to the conditions herein.
1.2 Proof of death must include an officially registered Death Certificate issued by a national registry and certified by a licensed medical practitioner.

## Section 2: Exclusions & Suicide Clause
2.1 Suicide Exclusion: If the Insured commits suicide within 12 months from the policy inception date, the company's liability is limited to the refund of premiums paid. No death benefit is payable.
2.2 Death arising from hazardous hobbies, war, or criminal activities of the Insured is excluded from the standard death benefit.

## Section 3: Contestability Window
3.1 Contestability Period: If the death of the Insured occurs within 24 months (2 years) from the inception date, the company has the right to contest the claim and audit all medical histories for pre-existing conditions.
3.2 Any misstatement of age, gender, or medical history identified during the contestability window can result in immediate claim denial or adjusted benefits.
