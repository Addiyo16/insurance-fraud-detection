# COMPREHENSIVE MOTOR INSURANCE POLICY

## Section 1: Damage & Total Loss Cover
1.1 The maximum liability of the company in respect of loss or damage to the vehicle shall be limited to the Insured Declared Value (IDV) specified in the policy schedule.
1.2 In the event of repair costs exceeding 75% of the IDV, the claim may be settled on a Constructive Total Loss (CTL) basis.
1.3 Repairs must be certified by an authorized workshop. Estimates from unaccredited garages are subject to inspection.

## Section 2: Exclusions & Restrictions
2.1 Loss or damage arising from theft is covered only if the vehicle is not active or registered as stolen prior to the reported incident under suspicious circumstances.
2.2 The insurer is not liable for claims where the driver did not hold a valid, active driving license at the time of the accident.
2.3 Mismatches between police FIR reports and driver declarations regarding accident location or severity are grounds for denial.

## Section 3: Verification & Auditing
3.1 The license plate number of the claimed vehicle must match the registered policy registry.
3.2 Incident reporting delays exceeding 72 hours without reasonable justification are subject to mandatory investigation and risk-scoring penalties.
